Grammar Guide

Sentence Pattern:

The + [animal] + is + [preposition] + the + [place].
ON: Animal is on top of the object.
Example: The cat is on the box.
IN: Animal is inside the object.
Example: The rat is in the box.
UNDER: Animal is below the object.
Example: The dog is under the chair.
